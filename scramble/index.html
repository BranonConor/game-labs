SCRAMBLE / FIELD GUIDE

Make a word.
Change the board.

Drag through side-sharing tiles, or tap them one by one. Move up, down, left, or right and turn as often as you like—no diagonal steps and no tile reused within one word. Your path needs at least four tiles, one existing letter, and one empty tile.

Type the missing letters in the input or directly from your keyboard; existing letters are skipped automatically. Enter submits a valid word and locks your new tiles. Esc clears the unsubmitted path and letters. Backspace removes your last typed letter. On a keyboard, use arrow keys to move around the board and Space to select tiles.

New letters score their printed Scrabble-style values plus two points each. Reused letters help you connect words, but do not score again. Some starting tiles carry a one-use effect: a pink ×2 doubles that word's score; a mint +5 adds five before doubling. Both are spent when a valid word crosses them. Each accepted route keeps its own color on the board.

Fill the board or keep building until the four-minute clock expires. You can only submit each word once.

The default board is the same for everyone on a given UTC day. DEV / RESEED makes a separate practice board with a fresh timer. Words are checked against a pinned, broad English word-game lexicon, including common inflections and regional spellings; this is not an official Scrabble dictionary. Your run is saved locally; no global ranking is active.
