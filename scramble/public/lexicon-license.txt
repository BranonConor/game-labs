This snapshot contains only ASCII alphabetic words of length 4 through 16.
It is an independent open word-game lexicon, not an official Scrabble word list.
The source combines US, UK, and Australian spellings and is refined for
Letterpress. Dictionary decisions should be versioned before competitive play.
